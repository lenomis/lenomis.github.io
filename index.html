About


I've spent 13 years building operational systems in environments that didn't have them yet.

At small nonprofits and a church organization, I owned digital communications end-to-end — websites, CRM, payments, registration — and built the full operational infrastructure for a first-time national conference from scratch. No playbook. Just a problem that needed solving.

At a large tech company, I moved into content design and UX — working across research, content strategy, instructional design, and video production. I learned how product teams think, how design and engineering talk (and don't talk) to each other, and where the operational gaps tend to live.

I also co-run a small business with my spouse, which means I've had real skin in the game on pricing, financials, and figuring out who we're actually trying to serve.

What ties all of it together isn't a job title — it's a pattern: I show up when something needs to be built, figured out, or connected, and I make it work.
